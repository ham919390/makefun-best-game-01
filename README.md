**Emberlight — Keeper of the Last Lantern.** An original canvas arcade game in vanilla
JavaScript, with no library and no build step. You steer a paper sky-lantern up through the
night: eat drifting embers to feed its flame, dodge the storm wisps that dive at it, brace
when the wind rises, and carry the light to the Dawn Line at 1,000 metres before the flame
gutters out. The flame always burns down — faster in each of the night's four watches — so
every ember is both score and life. Three wisp strikes, or an empty flame, ends the flight.

Steer with the arrow keys or WASD, or hold the mouse to guide her. P pauses, M mutes; every
sound is synthesised live with WebAudio. Title, HUD, pause, dawn and game-over screens, a
persistent best score, and deterministic `?shot=` scenes for screenshots are all included.
